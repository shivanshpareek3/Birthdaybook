A Letter For You

Happy 20th Birthday, Humari Suhani ❤️🎂

Aaj pata nahi kyun ye letter likhte waqt thoda emotional sa feel ho raha hai... shayad isliye kyunki meri Kiddo aaj officially 20 ki ho gayi hai. 🥹❤️ Sach bolu toh kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki time itni jaldi kaise nikal gaya. Aisa lagta hai jaise kal hi ki baat ho aur aaj hum tere 20th birthday par khade hain.

20 saal ho gaye madam ko duniya mein aaye hue, lekin harkatein dekh ke kabhi-kabhi lagta hai age abhi bhi wahi chhoti si butki wali hai. 😂 Aur ab 20 ki ho gayi ho toh please thodi maturity bhi dikha dena... although mujhe pata hai uski zyada umeed rakhna bekaar hai. 😭😂

Tu genuinely un logon mein se hai jo life mein aate hain aur pata hi nahi chalta ki kab itne important ban gaye. Tere saath kitni saari random baatein, hasi-mazaak, fights, gussa, nakhre, late-night talks aur stupid moments hain ki agar sab likhne baithun toh shayad ye book khatam ho jayegi, par memories nahi. ❤️

Aur tera gussa... uska toh alag hi chapter hona chahiye. 😂 Kabhi-kabhi aisa lagta hai ki madam ne 24/7 anger mode permanently ON kar rakha hai, aur phir thodi der baad khud hi normal bhi ho jaati ho. 😭😂 Honestly, funny bhi lagta hai aur cute bhi. Bas thoda kam gussa kiya kar, baaki sab badhiya hai.

Aur ek aur cheez jo mujhe kabhi samajh nahi aayegi—'bas 2 minute mein ready ho jaungi.' 😂 Suhani ke 2 minute ka actual meaning ab sabko pata hai... minimum 45 minutes. 💅🏻😭 Please 20 saal ki age mein is aadat ko thoda update kar lena.

A Letter For You (Continued)

But in sab masti ke beech ek cheez jo main genuinely maanta hoon, woh ye hai ki tu dil se bahut sweet hai. Upar se attitude, gussa, nakhre aur drama jitna marzi ho, but heart se tu bahut caring hai. Aur shayad isi wajah se tere saath rehna itna easy lagta hai.

Mujhe sabse zyada achha ye lagta hai ki tere saath mujhe kuch pretend nahi karna padta. Main jaisa hoon waisa reh sakta hoon. Hum bina kisi reason ke has sakte hain, stupid si baaton par lad sakte hain, ek dusre ko irritate kar sakte hain aur phir kuch time baad aise baat karte hain jaise kuch hua hi nahi. 😂❤️

Aur pata hai, teri ek chhoti si cheez mujhe bahut achhi lagti hai—jab tu mujhe 'Ansh' bolti hai. Pata nahi kyun, but tere muh se mera naam sunna hamesha thoda different feel hota hai. ❤️

Kab tu meri life ka itna important part ban gayi, honestly pata hi nahi chala. Ab teri calls, messages, random baatein, tera gussa, tera 'kuch nahi hua' wala kuch hua hota hai, aur tere random nakhre... in sab ki aadat si ho gayi hai. Kabhi-kabhi tu mujhe bahut pareshan karti hai, but phir agar tu baat na kare toh woh bhi ajeeb sa lagta hai. 😂❤️

Aur ye one year... pata nahi kaise nikal gaya. Is ek saal mein humne kitni chhoti-badi memories bana li hain. Har moment perfect nahi tha, obviously nahi ho sakta, hum dono ne ek dusre ko irritate bhi kiya hai, gussa bhi kiya hai, unnecessary arguments bhi hue hain... but shayad relationship ki beauty bhi isi mein hai. Har cheez perfect hone ki zarurat nahi hoti. Bas saamne wala important hona chahiye.

Aur tu mere liye important hai. Bahut zyada.

A Letter For You (Continued)

Kabhi-kabhi main sochta hoon life mein kitne log milte hain, kitne log chale jaate hain, but kuch log aise hote hain jo pata nahi kab life ka ek permanent sa part ban jaate hain. Tu mere liye exactly waisi hi hai.

Main nahi jaanta future mein life humein kahan le jayegi, kya-kya change hoga, hum kitne busy honge, kitni cheezein badlegi... but main genuinely ye chahta hoon ki chahe kuch bhi ho, tere face ki smile kabhi kam na ho. Aur jitna possible ho, apan hamesha saath rahein. ❤️

Tu jo bhi banna chahti hai, jo bhi achieve karna chahti hai, please karna. Kabhi khud par doubt ho toh thoda sa mujh par kar lena. Main tujhe yaad dila dunga ki tu kitni capable hai. ❤️

Main chahta hoon tera ye 20th year sirf birthday celebrate karne tak limited na rahe. Main chahta hoon tu naye experiences kare, naye places dekhe, bohot saari memories banaye, apne dreams ke close jaaye, khud par aur believe kare aur sabse important—khush rahe.

Aur jab life achhi ho, tab mere saath celebrate karna.

Aur jab life achhi na ho, tab mere paas aa jaana.

A Letter For You (Continued)

Tere saath baithne, tujhe sunne aur tere stupid problems ko unnecessarily seriously lene ke liye main hamesha ready rahunga. 😂❤️

I know main perfect nahi hoon. Kabhi stupid behave karta hoon, kabhi tujhe unnecessarily irritate karta hoon, kabhi woh nahi bol pata jo mujhe bolna chahiye. But ek cheez main chahta hoon ki tu hamesha yaad rakhe—

You matter to me. A lot.

Tere saath jo normal moments hote hain na, woh bhi mere liye special hote hain. Chahe hum kuch special kar rahe ho ya bas random si baat kar rahe ho, somehow woh moments yaad reh jaate hain.

Aur agar kabhi future mein hum ye birthday book wapas kholkar ye letter padhein, toh I hope hum dono ek dusre ko dekhein, smile karein aur bolein—

'Yaar, hum kitne cute aur stupid the.' 😂❤️

A Letter For You (Continued)

So meri Kiddo, welcome to 20. 🥹❤️

Ab officially badi ho gayi ho, but please zyada mature mat hona. Mujhe meri wahi cute, sundar, thodi pagal, thodi annoying, thodi gusse wali aur bahut zyada lovable Kiddo chahiye. 😂❤️

Bas aaj ke din main itna hi kehna chahta hoon—

Thank you for coming into my life.

Thank you for being you.

A Letter For You (Continued)

Aur thank you mujhe ye feeling dene ke liye ki life mein koi hai jiske saath main apni khushi bhi share karna chahta hoon aur apni problems bhi.

I hope tera ye naya year tujhe woh sab de jo tu deserve karti hai—happiness, peace, success, beautiful memories, achhe log, crazy trips, bohot saari hasi, thoda kam gussa... aur haan, bohot saara paisa bhi, kyunki emotional happiness apni jagah hai but shopping bhi toh zaroori hai. 😂💸

Aur ek last baat...
